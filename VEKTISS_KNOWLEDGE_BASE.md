## Common Questions & Answers

**Will this replace my receptionist or staff?**
Not necessarily. Vektiss Voice handles overflow, after-hours, and routine calls so your team can focus on higher-value work. Many clients keep their staff — the AI fills the gaps and handles what falls through the cracks.

**How long does setup take?**
Vektiss Voice is live within **48 hours** of kickoff. You don't do anything — we handle the entire build.

**Do I have to change my phone number?**
No. You simply forward your existing business number to the Vektiss line. Your number stays yours and nothing changes for your customers.

**Can it transfer calls to a real person?**
Yes. The AI can route calls to you or your team based on rules you define — emergencies go straight to your cell, VIP clients get routed to a specific person, sales calls go to sales.

**What industries does Vektiss work with?**
Home services, real estate, medical/wellness, professional services, law firms, auto repair, security companies, financial services, and more. If your business gets inbound calls and needs to capture leads, Vektiss Voice can help.

**Is there a contract?**
Terms vary by service. Our team will walk you through the specifics for your plan before you commit to anything.

**What does the $500 setup fee cover?**
Full configuration of your AI receptionist — custom scripts, call routing rules, intake form setup, email and SMS alert configuration, dashboard setup, testing, and go-live support. You don't touch a thing.

**Can the AI send intake forms?**
Yes — this is one of Vektiss Voice's most powerful features. The AI can text or email your intake form, application, or quote request directly to the caller while they're still on the phone. By the time you follow up, their paperwork is already done.

**Can I try it before buying?**
Yes — call **(346) 594-7686** right now to experience Vektiss Voice live. No signup required.

**What happens after the call?**
You receive an instant email alert with the caller's name, phone number, what they asked, the action taken (form sent, call routed, etc.), and a link to the full call recording and transcript. Everything is also logged in your dashboard automatically.

**Does it work after hours and on weekends?**
Yes — Vektiss Voice is on 24/7, including nights, weekends, and holidays. It never calls in sick.

**Can it handle multiple calls at once?**
Yes. Unlike a human receptionist, Vektiss Voice can handle simultaneous calls with no hold times.

**What languages does it support?**
English and Spanish are fully supported. Additional languages available on custom plans.

**Can I customize what the AI says?**
Yes — the scripts, personality, routing rules, and intake form flows are all customized to your business during setup.
